# Privacy Policy — Molar

_Effective date: 2026-05-17_

Molar is a toothbrushing timer for children. This document explains what data the app handles. The short version: **nothing leaves your device**.

## Data we collect

**None.** Molar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Specifically, the app does not:

- Create accounts or require sign-in.
- Read or store your name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
- Use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- Make any network requests. Molar works fully offline.

## Data stored on your device

Molar keeps no persistent user data on the device. The app's only state — the current brushing step and remaining seconds — exists only in memory while the app is running and is discarded when the app is closed.

## Children's privacy

Molar is designed for children. Because the app collects nothing, transmits nothing, and stores nothing, it does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and complies with COPPA and GDPR-K by virtue of collecting no data.

## Third-party services

There are no third-party services, SDKs, or analytics integrated into Molar.

## Permissions

Molar does not request any system permissions (no notifications, no microphone, no camera, no location, no contacts, no photo library).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new effective date.
